# Reference

* [Brann- og eksplosjonsvernloven (LOV-2002-06-14-20) — §§ 1, 5–8 og 13](lovdata-brann-eksplosjonsvernloven.md) - Primary Norwegian Fire and Explosion Protection Act provisions on purpose, general duties, owner/user duties, special fire objects, and municipal supervision.
* [Byggteknisk forskrift (TEK17, FOR-2017-06-19-840) — Kapittel 11 utvalgte paragrafer](lovdata-tek17-kap11.md) - Primary Norwegian Technical Building Regulations (TEK17) Chapter 11 fire safety provisions on risk classes, fire classes, escape routes, alarms, and manual extinguishing.
* [Forskrift om brannforebygging (FOR-2015-12-17-1710) — Kapittel 1–3](lovdata-brannforebygging.md) - Primary Norwegian fire prevention regulation covering general duties, building owner duties, and user/operator duties (§§ 1–13).
* [Internkontrollforskriften (FOR-1996-12-06-1127) — §§ 1–5](lovdata-internkontroll.md) - Primary Norwegian Internal Control Regulation provisions on systematic health, environment, and safety (HMS) work and documentation.
